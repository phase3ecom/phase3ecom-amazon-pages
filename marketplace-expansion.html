eBay AU

eBay AU — what it looks like

eBay AU is Australia's second-largest marketplace by buyer volume and has a distinctly different buyer demographic to Amazon. It skews toward value-conscious buyers, and categories like electronics, fashion, parts and accessories, and collectibles perform particularly strongly.

eBay's algorithm rewards listing freshness and seller feedback score. The listing format is different to Amazon — shorter titles, different image requirements, seller store branding.

What we handle
  • eBay Seller account setup
  • Product catalogue adaptation from Amazon listings
  • eBay-specific SEO (different keyword signals to Amazon)
  • eBay store setup and branding
  • Promoted Listings setup (eBay's ad format)
  • Seller feedback strategy
Catch

Catch — what it looks like

Catch has 5 million+ registered members and is owned by Wesfarmers (Kmart, Target, Bunnings parent company). It's particularly strong for household goods, health and wellness, toys, and consumer electronics. Catch buyers skew female, 25–45, and household-focused.

Catch operates on an invitation and approval basis — not all brands can list immediately. We handle the application and onboarding process.

What we handle
  • Catch marketplace application and approval
  • Product catalogue adaptation
  • Catch-specific compliance requirements
  • Ongoing listing management
International

International expansion

For brands ready to take their products beyond Australia, Amazon's international marketplaces are the most structured path. The US (Amazon.com), UK (Amazon.co.uk), and Asia-Pacific markets (Singapore, Japan) all operate within the same Seller Central environment with some configuration changes.

International expansion involves additional compliance requirements, currency considerations, FBA logistics differences, and market-specific pricing strategy. We assess this on a case-by-case basis — it's not right for every brand, and we won't recommend it until your AU marketplace presence is generating consistent returns.
