The problem

Why Seller Central stops most people before they start

Amazon Seller Central is built for scale, not for first-time sellers. The interface is dense, the compliance requirements are category-specific, and the documentation Amazon provides assumes you already know what you're doing.

🪪

Account verification

Takes days and requires specific documentation — business registration, bank statements, ID — in formats Amazon accepts. Getting this wrong delays launch and triggers account flags that are difficult to resolve.

🔢

GTINs (barcodes)

Required for most listings. Many AU small brands don't have GS1-registered GTINs. Getting exemptions or sourcing correctly is a process most sellers have to figure out on their own — and often get wrong.

⚖

Category & compliance

Varies by product type. Health and beauty may need ARTG registration. Electronics need AU compliance markings. Getting categorised incorrectly can trigger listing suppression or account health warnings.

🚚

FBA configuration

Choosing FBA vs FBM has direct implications for margins, Prime eligibility, and Buy Box win rate. Most new sellers pick one without understanding the tradeoffs — then spend months undoing the mistake.

The result: a half-built account, or a live listing that's technically wrong and will cost more to fix later than it would have cost to build correctly in the first place.

What we do

What a correct setup looks like

Seller Central account creation and verification

We guide the entire process — business registration, identity verification, bank setup, tax configuration. If you already have an account, we audit it for compliance issues before we build on it.

GTIN strategy

We assess whether your products need full GS1-registered barcodes, whether GTIN exemptions apply to your category, and source or advise accordingly. This is one of the most commonly botched steps in a DIY setup.

Category and compliance mapping

For every product you want to list, we identify the correct category, subcategory, and any AU-specific compliance requirements. Products that need ARTG numbers, specific AU standards markings, or category-specific approval are flagged before we start — not after.

FBA vs FBM assessment

We model the margin impact of FBA fees against your product dimensions, weight, and average selling price to recommend the right fulfilment model. For most AU brands, FBA provides significant advantages. For some product types, FBM makes more sense. We run the numbers.

FBA shipment creation

If we're setting up FBA, we create the first inbound shipment plan, generate labels, and manage the process of getting your stock into Amazon's fulfilment network. We tell you exactly what to send, how to package it, and where to ship it.

Brand Registry application

If you have a registered trademark, we initiate Brand Registry — giving you access to A+ Content, Brand Storefront, and brand protection tools. If you don't have a trademark yet, we advise on the process and set up a standard seller account in the interim.

Account health baseline

Before launch, we run through Amazon's account health checklist — performance metrics, policy compliance, listing quality — to make sure there are no flags that will affect your visibility from day one.

FAQ

Common questions

I already started an account but got stuck. Can you take it over? +
Yes. We audit the existing account first, fix any issues, and continue from wherever you are. Existing accounts sometimes have compliance flags from an incomplete setup — we clear these before we build on them.
My products are in a restricted category. Can you still help? +
Restricted categories (health, food, electronics, toys and certain others) require category-specific approval from Amazon. We manage the approval application as part of setup. Approval timelines vary — Amazon typically responds within 1–5 business days, but some categories take longer.
I use Shopify. Do I have to change how I manage my inventory? +
Your Shopify store stays exactly as it is. We set up Amazon as a separate, parallel channel. If you want inventory sync between Shopify and Amazon Seller Central, we can advise on integration tools. This isn't required to get started, but it's useful once you have consistent Amazon volume.
What's the minimum number of products I need? +
We recommend at least 5 SKUs. Fewer than that limits your ability to run Sponsored Brands ads and makes it harder to build catalogue breadth. If you have fewer than 5 SKUs, talk to us — there are ways to structure the launch that still work.
