Why it's harder than it looks

Where DIY Amazon setup breaks down

There are more components to getting listed and live on Amazon than most first-time sellers expect. Getting any one of these wrong can delay your launch — or worse, get your account suspended or listings suppressed.

Account creation and verification

Requires specific documentation — business registration, bank statements, identity documents — in formats Amazon accepts. Common delays: wrong document type, unverified business address, or bank account mismatch. Getting this wrong suspends the account before you've even started.

Brand trademark application

Required to unlock Amazon Brand Registry, A+ Content, and brand protection tools. The application process takes 6–9 months in Australia. We advise on timing and manage the interim setup so you're not blocked while you wait for registration.

Amazon Brand Registry

Unlocks the full suite of brand tools: A+ Content, Brand Storefront, Sponsored Brands ads, and IP protection. Only accessible once you hold a registered trademark. We initiate the application and walk you through the requirements step by step.

GTIN (barcode) sourcing

Required for most Amazon listings. Many AU store owners don't have GS1-registered GTINs. Getting exemptions or sourcing correctly is a process most sellers figure out the hard way — and often get wrong the first time, triggering listing suppression or category mismatches.

Technical and safety compliance

Varies by product type and category. Health and beauty may need ARTG registration. Electronics need AU compliance markings. Getting categorised incorrectly triggers listing suppression or account health warnings — sometimes weeks after you think everything is live.

Attribute mapping

Amazon's catalogue requires precise attribute matching — variation themes, browse node placement, search term fields. A mismatch at this stage affects how your listings rank, which keywords you're eligible for, and whether your products appear in the right category pages.

With all these components to consider, it's easy for a first-time seller to make a mistake somewhere. Rejections and suppressions are common. We know the pitfalls — and we'll have you up and live in 30 days.
