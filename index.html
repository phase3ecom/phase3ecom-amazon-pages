FAQ

Common questions

I already have a Shopify store. Do I need to change anything? +
No. Amazon runs alongside your Shopify store, not instead of it. Amazon becomes your acquisition channel — it finds buyers you can't reach through your own site. Your Shopify store becomes the retention engine — email, loyalty, repeat purchase. The two work together.
What if I already have an Amazon seller account? +
We can work with an existing account. We'll audit it first to identify any compliance issues, listing problems, or account health flags before we build on top of it.
What happens after the 30-day launch? +
Your products are live, but that's the start. Months 2–3 focus on review velocity, pricing, and your first ad spend. Month 4+ is where ranking improves, the catalogue expands, and the channel starts compounding. We stay involved through ongoing monthly management.
Can you add eBay and Catch as well? +
Yes, from Month 4 onwards. We treat Amazon as the primary marketplace first — get it working, then expand. eBay AU and Catch are included in the Phase 03 roadmap as optional add-ons.
What products don't work on Amazon AU? +
Categories with heavy regulatory requirements, restricted listings, or very low AU search volume need case-by-case assessment. We'll flag these before we start. We won't take your $1,500 if your product category isn't viable.
How much should I budget for Amazon PPC? +
For a new seller with 5–10 SKUs, a starting budget of $500–$1,500/month for ad spend is realistic. We build the strategy around your budget — we don't inflate spend to inflate our management scope.
