The problem

What plain-text product pages are costing you

Amazon's default product description is a plain-text field. Most buyers scroll past it. The ones who don't are usually looking for a reason to buy — or a reason not to.

A+ Content replaces that field with image-and-copy modules: comparison tables, lifestyle imagery, feature callouts, brand story sections. It's the closest thing Amazon gives you to a landing page.

Sellers with A+ Content see an average lift in conversion rate. Sellers without it are competing against listings that have it — and losing at the decision point.

The brand story mistake

Your DTC story doesn't translate directly

Many Shopify brands think their DTC brand story translates directly to Amazon. It doesn't. Amazon buyers don't read long copy. They scan. They compare. They look at images first, then bullet points, then A+ Content last.

What you write there needs to answer the lingering objections of a buyer who is almost sold — not introduce your brand from scratch.

Brand Storefront

Your branded space inside Amazon

The Amazon Brand Storefront is your branded space within Amazon — a multi-page store that buyers land on when they click your brand name. It showcases your full catalogue, tells your brand story, and provides a place to send external traffic (from Meta or Google) that Amazon's algorithm rewards.

We structure the storefront, write the copy for each page, and brief the design. Submitted and managed through Seller Central.

Requirements

Brand Registry — do you need it?

You need Brand Registry to access A+ Content and Brand Storefront. Brand Registry requires a registered trademark — in Australia, this means an IP Australia trademark.

If you don't have a trademark yet, we can advise on the process. It typically takes 6–9 months to register in Australia. There are workarounds for interim access to some A+ features — we'll walk you through them.

If you have a trademark already, Brand Registry setup is included in our TX Marketplace Accelerator setup phase.

Strategic connection

Storefront + external traffic

Your Brand Storefront has a specific strategic role beyond just branding. When you run Meta or Google campaigns and send external traffic to your Amazon Storefront URL, Amazon tracks those sessions separately and rewards the organic ranking of your products.

This is the Phase 03 cross-channel play that compounds your results over time — connecting your Shopify advertising to your Amazon ranking.
