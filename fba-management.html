Comparison

Which fulfilment model is right for your products?

Not every product belongs in FBA. Understanding the difference matters before you commit.

Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA)
  • Amazon stores your stock in their AU fulfilment centres
  • Amazon picks, packs, and ships every order
  • All customer service and returns handled by Amazon
  • Products receive the Prime badge
  • Higher Buy Box win rate (Amazon favours FBA sellers)

FBA fees apply: fulfilment fee (based on size/weight) + monthly storage fee

Best for: Products with consistent sales velocity, high Buy Box competition, manageable size/weight relative to selling price

Fulfilment by Merchant (FBM)
  • You store and ship stock yourself (or through a 3PL)
  • You handle customer service and returns
  • No Prime badge (unless Seller Fulfilled Prime — strict requirements)
  • Lower Buy Box win rate in most categories

No FBA fees, but you carry fulfilment cost

Best for: Oversized items where FBA fees are disproportionate, low-velocity products, or products with complex customer service requirements

The margin calculation matters. An item that costs $4 to ship yourself via FBM might cost $6.50 in FBA fees — but if FBA status lifts your conversion rate enough to make it up, the net result is positive. We run this calculation for your products before recommending either option.

What FBA actually costs

FBA fees in Australia are calculated per unit sold, based on product size and weight. There are two main components.

Fulfilment fee (per unit shipped)

Covers picking, packing, and delivery. Varies by product size tier. As a rough guide for standard-size products:

Size tierApprox. fee per unit
Small items (under 300g)~$4.50–$6.50
Medium items (300g–1kg)~$6.50–$9.00
Larger itemsScaled by weight and dimensions

Monthly storage fee

Charged per cubic metre of inventory stored. Standard rate applies most of the year, with a peak surcharge October–December.

These fees come on top of Amazon's referral fee (typically 8–15% of the selling price, depending on category). We factor all of this into margin modelling before you send a unit.

Exact fees depend on product specifications. We calculate yours specifically — not from averages.
