The problem

What a bad listing actually costs you

A poorly optimised listing doesn't just sit quietly. It actively works against you.

Amazon's A10 algorithm uses your listing's keywords, conversion rate, and sales velocity to decide where you rank. A listing with weak copy, misplaced keywords, or the wrong category doesn't just rank low — it takes longer to climb even when you start advertising.

Every day a product is live with a substandard listing is a day you're paying more for PPC to achieve what a well-built listing would do organically.

Most common mistakes

Shopify brands new to Amazon typically:

  • Write titles for the brand, not for search
  • Use bullet points that describe features, not benefits or search terms
  • Leave backend keywords blank or fill with irrelevant terms
  • Set category to whatever was easiest to find
  • Apply US or UK data to AU search behaviour
What we do

What listing optimisation actually means

🔍

Keyword research — Australia-specific

We don't use US keyword data for Australian listings. AU search volumes are lower and the terms people use are different. We build keyword maps from AU-specific search data, then layer them across title, bullets, description, and backend fields in the order Amazon's algorithm weights them.

📝

Title structure

Amazon's title is the highest-weighted field for ranking. We build titles that satisfy the algorithm (keyword-dense, correctly formatted for your category) and convert the human reading them. Both matter — most sellers optimise for one and ignore the other.

⚡

Bullet points

Five bullet points. Each carries secondary keywords and addresses a specific buyer objection or benefit. Not a marketing brochure — a conversion tool. Every character counted.

🔧

Backend search terms

The fields buyers never see but the algorithm always reads. We fill every character, without repetition, with terms that capture search traffic your title and bullets can't accommodate.

🎨

A+ Content

For Brand Registry holders, A+ Content replaces the plain-text description with image-and-copy modules that lift conversion rate. We write the copy and brief the design. See the A+ Content page →

🗂

Category and browse tree

Getting your product into the right category and subcategory affects which browse nodes you appear in and which competitors you're ranked against. Most new sellers set this wrong at setup — we correct it.
