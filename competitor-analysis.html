Part 2 — Messaging Analysis

Hero headline patterns

❌ Formula 1 — Outcome promise (most common, most generic)
  • "Drive Sales on the World's Largest Marketplace" — Merge
  • "Become a Power Seller on Amazon" — First Page
  • "Unlock Unstoppable Amazon Growth" — Export Accelerator
  • "Maximising Product Visibility and Profits" — Krolog

Saturated language. Every agency says this. Avoid.

⚠ Formula 2 — Positioning/identity (more differentiated)
  • "The Only Amazon Agency You Will Ever Need" — SalesDuo
  • "eCommerce Management Agency" — RocketAMS
  • "Full Service Agency Specialising in Amazon" — Unmaze

Category-defining, not brand-defining. Okay but not distinctive enough.

✅ Formula 3 — Problem-led (strongest for conversion)
  • "Your products are awesome, but not selling fast?" — Krolog
  • "Shoppers are searching. Your products should be the answer." — Impressive
  • "Commerce moves fast, Sequence moves faster." — Sequence

Leads with the buyer's problem or context. Most effective for conversion.

⚠ Formula 4 — Credibility-first
  • "Founded by ex-Amazon AU employees" — RocketAMS, Simple Sellers, Unmaze, SalesDuo

Only compelling if the claim is real and specific. Don't use if it's not true.

Phase 3 Ecom Amazon page direction:
Lead with the integrated angle — Amazon as a growth channel that works harder when it's connected to the rest of the business. Frame: "Your Amazon channel, built to work with your whole business."

Value proposition patterns

What almost everyone says — avoid these:
Data-driven approach Tailored strategies Measurable results Transparent reporting Partner, not just an agency End-to-end solutions We take ownership

What fewer people say well — worth adapting

SalesDuo: "Most agencies manage your account. SalesDuo scales your business." — Great contrast framing
Impressive: "Google and Meta generate awareness. Amazon closes the sale." — Great platform positioning
Australian Digital: "The person who pitches and the person delivering the work are the same person." — Accountability angle
Unmaze: "No empty promises or BS; we walk the walk." — Refreshing directness
Phase 3 Ecom existing (keep): "You don't need more advice. You need execution." — This is strong.
Integrated positioning — no competitor owns this: Phase 3 Ecom is the only agency in this space positioning Amazon as one channel in a connected ecom system alongside Shopify, email automation, Meta, and Google. All 30 competitors are Amazon-first or Amazon-only. Phase 3 Ecom is ecom-first.

Process frameworks

Most agencies show a 3–5 step process. Common patterns across market:

Audit → Strategy → Implement → Optimise — most common
Discover → Focus → Recommend → Deliver — 20twenty
12-Month Plan → Visibility → Hands-Off Engine → Protection — SalesDuo
Phase 3 Ecom: Run → Drive → Fly is already distinctive. The 3-phase framing maps cleanly to Amazon context (Launch → Grow → Compound). Adapt it — don't replace it.

Visual elements worth noting

  • Comparison tables (us vs. typical agency) — SalesDuo uses this well
  • Platform logos strip — Phase 3 Ecom already does this
  • Before/after metrics — most compelling social proof visual
  • Dashboard screenshots — SalesDuo, LION show proprietary tools
  • Founder/team photos — Australian Digital, Giant Horizons, Phase 3 Ecom (existing) — builds trust
Part 5 — Competitive Gaps

What no competitor is saying — own these

1

Amazon as part of your revenue ecosystem

Every competitor treats Amazon in isolation. Phase 3 Ecom can say: "Your Amazon store only works as hard as the ecosystem behind it. We connect it to your Shopify, your email flows, your ad strategy — so every channel feeds the next."

2

The $10K–$100K/month founder who's been burned by specialists

Most agencies target either new sellers or enterprise brands. The mid-market ecom owner — doing real revenue but stuck — is underserved. Phase 3 Ecom already speaks this language on the main site.

3

Execution vs. advice framing applied to Amazon

"You've probably read every Amazon guide. You know what FBA is. You know A+ content matters. What you need is someone to do it — and connect it to everything else you're building."

4

Brisbane-based — specific geographic pride

Most AU-wide agencies don't claim a home. Being Brisbane-based and proud of it is a differentiator, especially for QLD clients.

5

No lock-in + guarantee

Project E and RocketAMS do this. Phase 3 Ecom should match or beat: month-to-month, execution guaranteed.

What Phase 3 Ecom already has that competitors don't

  • Broader ecom system (Shopify + Email + Ads + Amazon = one connected strategy)
  • The TX Sales Method / Run-Drive-Fly framework
  • Named client results with ROI framing ($1→$X.XX)
  • Clear target audience ($10K–$100K/month)
  • "Not taught. Executed." positioning
  • Explicit guarantee language ("We guarantee execution")
Part 6 — Content Recommendations

Hero section

Frame Amazon as a growth channel that works harder when it's connected to the rest of the business.

Headline options

"Your Amazon channel, built to work with everything else."
"Amazon isn't separate from your ecom strategy. It should drive it."
"Most brands treat Amazon like a side channel. We treat it like a revenue engine."
"You're already doing the work. Your Amazon channel should be doing it too."

Subheadline direction

"We set up, manage and optimise your Amazon presence as part of your full ecom system — not as an afterthought."
"Listing optimisation, ad management, and A+ content — executed and connected to your Shopify, email, and paid strategy."

Services to describe

Core (must have)

  • Amazon Account Setup & Management
  • Product Listing Optimisation
  • A+ Content & Brand Storefront
  • Amazon Advertising
  • Keyword Research & Ranking
  • Performance Reporting

Differentiators (Phase 3 Ecom specific)

  • Amazon × Shopify Integration
  • Amazon × Email Automation (external traffic to listings)
  • Amazon × Meta/Google (full-funnel awareness → Amazon close)
  • Marketplace Expansion (eBay, Bunnings, TheIconic)

Process section — adapt Run-Drive-Fly

Phase 1 — Foundation (Run)

Account health check, listings built to rank and convert, FBA/fulfilment set up correctly, Brand Registry established.

Phase 2 — Growth (Drive)

Amazon Ads launched and optimised, A+ content live, external traffic from Meta/Google driving to listings, keyword rankings improving.

Phase 3 — Scale (Fly)

Expanding to additional marketplaces, DSP/retargeting layer, international expansion, review generation, connected to the full ecom revenue system.

Social proof

  • Use the ROI format ($1 → $X.XX) — more visceral than % claims
  • Name the business and the outcome: "[Client] went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]."
  • Add Amazon-specific metrics once clients are on platform: ACoS%, ranking improvements, organic sales %

Points of difference

1. Amazon + everything else, connected — not a standalone service
2. Execution, not advice — we do the work, you see the results
3. One team, one strategy — your Amazon manager talks to your email strategist and your ads buyer
4. $10K–$100K/month specialists — we don't take clients we can't move
5. Month-to-month, no lock-in — we keep clients by delivering, not by contract
6. Brisbane-based, AU-market experts — GST, AU compliance, AU consumer behaviour

CTA patterns

Primary — keep existing: "Book a Call" ✅

Secondary options worth testing

  • "Get a Free Amazon Audit" — Project E, SalesDuo, Impressive all use audit offers
  • "See Our Amazon Results" — anchors to case studies
  • "Start with a Strategy Session" — consistent with TX Method framing
Avoid: "Get a Free Quote" — cheapens the service. Phase 3 Ecom sells strategy + execution, not a commodity.

FAQ topics

  • Do I need to already be on Amazon to work with you?
  • How does Amazon fit with my Shopify store?
  • What's the difference between FBA and FBM — which do you recommend?
  • How long before I see results on Amazon?
  • What does Amazon management cost?
  • Do you handle compliance, Brand Registry, and account health?
  • Can you manage Amazon alongside my other marketing?
